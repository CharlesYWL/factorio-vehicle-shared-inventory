---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-08-16
  Features:
    - Automatically moves construction materials from the character inventory into the trunk of the
      vehicle being ridden, so personal construction robots can build without leaving the vehicle
    - Supports spidertrons, cars and tanks (including modded vehicles of those prototype types)
    - Handles entity ghosts, tile ghosts, item request proxies and upgrade orders
    - Lends construction robots up to the vehicle roboport capacity whenever there is construction
      or deconstruction work nearby, highest quality first
    - Supplies cliff explosives for cliffs marked for deconstruction
    - Reclaims deconstruction spoils back into the player inventory when the trunk runs low on free
      slots, limited to surplus above a boarding baseline so the vehicle's own stock is never taken
    - Quality aware: items of different qualities are tracked separately
    - Borrowed items are returned when leaving the vehicle; the vehicle's own stock is never taken,
      and ammo, fuel and robots are never reclaimed
  Bugfixes:
    - Overflow reclaim no longer steals the vehicle's original stock or already-satisfied
      construction materials
    - Item moves preserve durability, spoilage, blueprint contents and nested inventories
    - Deconstruction queries now ignore other forces' orders and include tile deconstruction
    - Per-player settings for update interval, robot sharing, return-on-exit, overflow reclaim
      threshold and roboport requirement
    - /vsi-debug command reports tracking state, roboport radius, nearby work and computed needs
